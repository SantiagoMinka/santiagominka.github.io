Rules

Scalar. Increment L before each event (local or send). A message carries L. On receipt: L = max(L, ts) + 1. Then e → e′ ⇒ L(e) < L(e′), but not the opposite.

Vector. Local event or send at Pi: V[i]++. A message carries V. On receipt: V[j] = max(V[j], t[j]) for all j, then V[i]++. Then e → e′ ⇔ V(e) < V(e′), and e ∥ e′ ⇔ neither is smaller.

Total order. Break ties of scalar clocks with the process id: 3.1 < 3.2.
