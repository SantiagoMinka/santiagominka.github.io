How to read the result

Rules. On the first token: record the state, send a token on every outgoing channel, then record every other incoming channel. A message that arrives on a channel that is being recorded goes into that channel's record. A token on a channel stops its recording.

Assumptions. FIFO, reliable channels; the graph is strongly connected; no new application messages are sent while the snapshot runs.

Check. The total of recorded states and recorded channel messages is always 75. That is the total of the system (86) minus the messages that A, B and E sent after they recorded their state (3 on A→B, 4 on B→C, 4 on E→F). Different delivery orders give different snapshots, but all of them are consistent cuts.
