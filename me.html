About

Santiago Arévalo Gómez

Engineering student at Politecnico di Milano, splitting my time between coursework, building software, and training.

I like understanding how things work from the ground up, and I strongly believe that education must be open and accessible to everyone. In my honest opinion, the way we learn is broken, we get too much theory but we don't actually really see how things work both in practice and in our heads, that's why these notes contain mainly visualizations and interactive exercises, so that you can see how things work and learn by doing. I'm a software engineer and I love the idea of translating ideas into something tangible (not only code!)

Outside of school and work, you'll usually find me at the gym or on the mats doing jiu-jitsu.
